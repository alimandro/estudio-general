Estúdio do General

Estúdio do General is a personal desktop tool used by the owner of the football channel @generaldofut on TikTok to prepare short videos of his own and send them to his own TikTok account as a draft, so he can review and publish them from the TikTok mobile app.

What it does with TikTok

  • Login Kit: the owner signs in once with his own TikTok account to authorize the app.
  • Content Posting API (upload to inbox): the app uploads a video that the owner created to his own TikTok inbox/drafts. The owner opens TikTok and decides whether to post it.

The app runs only on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and does not post anything on its own on behalf of other people.
