Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Estúdio do General is a personal tool used only by its owner, on his own computer. It is not offered to other people and does not collect data from visitors or from other TikTok users.

What is stored

  • When the owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit), the access token that TikTok returns is stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file. It is used to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own TikTok drafts.
  • Basic account information (user.info.basic) is used only to confirm which of the owner's own accounts is connected.

What is not done

  • No data is sold, shared or sent to third parties.
  • No data of other TikTok users is collected.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking.

Videos

Videos are uploaded from the owner's computer directly to TikTok. The owner publishes them manually in the TikTok app.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services), or by deleting the local file with the token.

Contact

Through the TikTok profile @generaldofut.

Política de Privacidade (português)

O "Estúdio do General" é uma ferramenta pessoal, usada só pelo dono, no computador dele. Não coleta dados de visitantes nem de outros usuários do TikTok. O token de acesso que o TikTok devolve no login fica guardado apenas no computador do dono, num arquivo local, e serve só para enviar os vídeos dele como rascunho para o TikTok dele. Nada é vendido, compartilhado ou enviado a terceiros. O acesso pode ser revogado a qualquer momento nas configurações do TikTok. Contato: pelo perfil @generaldofut no TikTok.
